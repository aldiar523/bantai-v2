How a contract is marked

A

An official record

An audit, a court case, an official statement or a revoked licence already names the contract or its contractor.

B

A rule is broken

The figures contradict a law or each other: an award above the approved budget, a contract above the licence limit.

C

Several signals at once

Three or more independent signs point the same way: tender, price, contractor's record, site.

B and C marks and every decision are for the review team only. A person decides what happens next. Read the full method, tests and data sources →
